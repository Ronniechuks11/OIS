From first day to IGCSE

Three stages, one continuous Cambridge pathway — each one building directly on the last, in the same building, with the same faces in the hallway.

PreAges 3–5

Preschool

Early English immersion through play, songs, and a steady daily routine — the goal is comfort and confidence in the language before Primary asks anything more formal of it.

  • Daily English immersion through songs, stories, and play
  • Fine motor and pre-literacy groundwork
  • Shared assembly and playground time with the wider school
P1–P6Ages 5–11

Primary

The full Cambridge Primary curriculum — English, Maths, and Science as the core, wrapped in cross-curricular inquiry projects that pull in art, geography, and history along the way.

  • Cambridge Primary: English, Mathematics, Science
  • Cross-curricular inquiry projects each term
  • Danish language lessons woven through the week
S7–S11Ages 11–16

Secondary

Cambridge Secondary building toward IGCSE, with subject specialisation increasing each year and French introduced in the upper years alongside continued Danish.

  • Cambridge Secondary through to IGCSE examinations
  • French introduced in the upper years
  • Subject specialisation increases year over year

Where students go after IGCSE

OIS runs through age 16, so IGCSE is the end of our pathway rather than the middle of it. From there, students typically move on to an IB Diploma programme, the Danish gymnasium system, or a vocational track — whichever fits where the family is headed next.

A

IB Diploma Programme

The most common route for families staying on an international track.

B

Danish gymnasium

For students settling into the Danish system long-term.

C

Vocational routes

Available depending on the student's interests and plans.
