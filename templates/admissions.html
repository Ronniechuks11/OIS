Frequently asked questions

What language is instruction in?

English, throughout the entire school day, from Preschool through Secondary. Danish is taught as an additional subject.

Is there a school bus or canteen?

No — tuition does not currently include bus service or a canteen. Most families arrange their own transport and packed lunches.

[Confirm with the school: many international schools welcome local families too, alongside those relocating for work — worth stating clearly here once confirmed.]

When can we apply?

Admissions are rolling — you can inquire at any point in the school year, and placement depends on space in the relevant grade.
