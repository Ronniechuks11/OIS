Two schools, one courtyard

OIS is the international department of Henriette Hørlücks Skole, a Danish school founded in 1870. Children share morning assembly and the playground, then follow entirely separate curricula — Cambridge in English on one side, the Danish Folkeskole system on the other.

Settling into Odense

Denmark's third-largest city is compact, bike-first, and built around Odense University Hospital and the University of Southern Denmark — which means a steady flow of relocating families already navigating the same move yours is making.

  • Short, mostly-by-bike commutes across the whole city
  • A large existing international community around SDU and OUH
  • A comparatively straightforward relocation process for families
